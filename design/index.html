Cheltenham Data · Design directions · 5 Aug 2026

Three ways out of the template.

Same content, same palette, same 3D and animation budget. Each direction breaks a different one of the habits that make a page read as machine-made: centred symmetry, uniform rounded cards, even whitespace, and decoration that isn't carrying information.

Direction A

Ledger — the printed commercial report

Everything hangs on a visible twelve-column grid. Hairline rules and leader dots replace cards; figures are tabular and ranged right in a money column. The 3D element is a stack of paper that tips towards your cursor, not a floating glass panel.

Reads as a chartered analyst who sends you a document, not a landing page.

 Direction B

Instrument — the tool, not the brochure

Dense, monospaced and unafraid of small text: a toolbar, a fixed index rail, key/value records and sparklines on every figure. The 3D element pulls one chart apart into its three layers — grid, series, result — and lets you turn the stack.

Reads as someone who builds the dashboards, shown in their own idiom.

 Direction C

Workshop — sheets on a desk, marked up by hand

Printed sheets propped and overlapping, each a degree off true, with a brass pin holding one down. The oak in the palette becomes marker pen: a circle round the headline, an arrow to the number that matters, drawn on as you scroll.

Reads as a person at a desk — the warmest of the three, and the least corporate.

Each mockup carries the real copy and the real figures across a header, hero, results deck, figures band, one case study and the footer — enough to judge the direction. The remaining sections (services, method, experience, AI policy, areas, FAQ, notes, contact) get built out once you pick one. These pages are noindex and not linked from the live site.
