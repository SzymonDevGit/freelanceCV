Yes, I use AI. Here's exactly how.

You deserve to know this up front rather than discover it later. AI is a tool I use to work faster — never a substitute for the analysis, the judgement or the accountability you're paying for. Every deliverable is reviewed, tested and understood by me before it reaches you, and I can explain any line of it.

Where AI helps
  • 01Writing and refactoring code. Scripts I then read, test and validate against real data before anything ships.
  • 02Drafting documentation and SOPs. First drafts I rewrite for your context, terminology and audience.
  • 03Exploring approaches faster. Pressure-testing options so you get a better solution in less time.
  • 04Building AI into your workflows. When that is the actual deliverable you've asked for.
Where it doesn't
  • —Your data doesn't go into public AI tools. Client data stays in your environment unless you explicitly agree otherwise in writing.
  • —No unreviewed output. Nothing generated is handed over without me verifying it against source data.
  • —No AI-invented numbers. Every figure in a report traces back to a source you can audit.
  • —The accountability is mine. If something is wrong, that's on me to find and fix — not on a model.

Working in regulated financial services taught me to treat data handling seriously — DSARs, anonymisation, erasure, FCA and Cyber Essentials audits. The same standard applies to how I use AI on your work. If you have an AI policy of your own, send it over and I'll work inside it.

Before you get in touch.

Do you only work with businesses in Cheltenham?
No. I'm based in Cheltenham and happy to work on site across Gloucestershire and the Cotswolds — Gloucester, Stroud, Tewkesbury, Cirencester, Worcester, Evesham — as well as Bristol and Bath. Most work is done remotely, so I take on clients anywhere in the UK.
I'm a small business. Is this overkill?
Usually the opposite. Small and growing businesses are where a few days of automation work pays back fastest, because nobody has had time to build proper systems yet. If you have people spending hours a week in spreadsheets, that's exactly the problem I solve — and it's cheaper than the headcount you'd otherwise add.
Are you available while working full time?
Yes — I take on a limited number of freelance projects alongside my role, which is exactly why I scope carefully and quote fixed timelines. If a project needs more capacity than I honestly have, I'll say so at the scoping call rather than take it on and miss a deadline.
How do you price work?
Fixed price per project, agreed in writing before I start. You'll know the cost, the deliverable and the deadline up front. For open-ended or ongoing work, a monthly retainer with a defined scope. No hourly billing surprises.
Who owns what you build?
You do. Code, dashboards, models and documentation are handed over in full at the end of the project, written to be maintained by your team. No lock-in and no dependency on me to keep it running.
How do you handle sensitive or regulated data?
I've handled regulated financial customer data daily, including DSAR requests, anonymisation and erasure, and contributed to FCA and Cyber Essentials audits. I'll work inside your existing controls, sign an NDA or DPA, and keep data in your environment. See the AI policy above for specifics on AI tooling.
Do you use AI on client work?
Yes, and I disclose exactly how — the full policy is in the section above. In short: AI helps me write and refactor code, draft documentation and explore approaches faster. Your data doesn't go into public AI tools, nothing generated is handed over unreviewed, and every figure in a report traces back to a source you can audit.
What happens on the first call?
You walk me through the process that's costing you time or money. I ask questions, and by the end you'll have an honest view of whether it's worth fixing, roughly what it would take, and whether I'm the right person for it. Free, no obligation, about thirty minutes.

Let's find the hours your business is losing.

Tell me about the process that's driving you mad. If I can fix it, I'll tell you how. If I can't, I'll tell you that too.
