    85% of UK data job ads don't tell you the salary

    AI hallucination rates went up, not down

    157 models compared across two snapshots of Vectara's leaderboard. The mean rate rose from 6.25% to 10.24%, flagship models got worse than their predecessors, and summary length now predicts hallucination when it used to tell you nothing.
